Workflow

How Nyay Setu AI Works

A seamless 6-step process from claim submission to final decision.

01
📤

Farmer Uploads Claim

Farmer submits claim with photos and details through the portal.

02
🤖

AI Analyzes Damage

Gemini AI processes images and generates a damage assessment report.

03
🌦️

Weather & Fraud Check

OpenWeather API cross-checks conditions; fraud patterns are flagged.

04
👮

Officer Reviews

Government officer reviews AI evidence and makes an informed decision.

05
⛓️

Blockchain Records

Decision is recorded immutably on Ethereum Sepolia via smart contract.

06
✅

Farmer Gets Status

Farmer receives final decision with full audit trail and reasoning.
