✦Know before you file

Crop cover, explained without the paperwork maze.

Use this quick guide to understand common PMFBY crop groups, insured risks, and the first steps after crop loss. Final eligibility depends on your notified crop, area, and policy.

72
hours can matter

For localized calamity and post-harvest loss, notify the insurer or relevant authority quickly—typically within 72 hours.

Common notified crop groups under PMFBY:

🌾 Cereals 🌿 Millets 🫛 Pulses 🌻 Oilseeds 🍅 Horticulture 🧺 Commercial crops

Coverage varies by state, season, notified area, and crop.

Common insured events may include:

  • ☀Drought / dry spellStanding crop and mid-season impact
  • ≋Flood / inundationWidespread or localized damage
  • ◌Hailstorm / cycloneWeather-led crop loss
  • ⌁Pest / diseaseNon-preventable notified risk
